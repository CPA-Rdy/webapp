CPALE Ready

A study tracker built for CPA Licensure Examination (CPALE) review.

CPALE Ready helps CPA board exam candidates organize and stay on top of their review. It's a personal study companion — not an official review course — designed to keep your preparation structured in the months leading up to the exam.

What it does

  • Subject & topic tracking — check off topics across all CPALE subjects as you complete them.
  • Study calendar — plan sessions and rest days against your actual exam date.
  • Scoresheet — log mock exam and preboard scores to track your trend over time.
  • Achievements & study history — a record of consistency to keep you motivated.
  • AI Study Coach — ask questions about your pacing and progress.
  • Your own data, your own backup — everything is stored locally in your browser by default, with optional backup to a file or to your own Google Drive. Nothing is stored on our servers.

Who it's for

CPALE candidates who want a lightweight, private way to track their review progress across the full board exam syllabus, without handing their study data to a third-party server.
